FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Kya isme Arduino board included hai?

Haan, dono kits (Allen 2.0 & Allen 3.0 Pro) me ek real Arduino Uno board included hai — kuch alag se kharidna nahi padta.

Kis age group ke bachchon ke liye sahi hai?

Ye kits beginners ke liye design kiye gaye hain — school-going kids jo pehli baar electronics/coding sikh rahe hain, unke liye best hain. Chhote bachchon ke liye parent/teacher ki guidance recommended hai.

Coding ka pehle se experience chahiye kya?

Nahi. Manual me step-by-step instructions hain — drag-drop blocks se shuru karke Arduino code tak, sab kuch guided hai.

Delivery me kitna time lagta hai?

Order confirm hone ke baad WhatsApp par exact delivery timeline bata di jaati hai. Kisi bhi update ke liye WhatsApp support par directly puchh sakte hain.
